## Dramatic Sky Ride 0.2.7

This small Generation II presentation update removes a ground-level effect
that could appear underneath airborne mounts in Pokemon Gold.

### Fixed

- Tall-grass rustle no longer appears when moving over grass during Flight.
- The effect is suppressed only while Flight is active.
- Native grass state, encounters, collisions, walking, Ground Ride and Visible
  Surf behavior remain unchanged.

The attached ZIP is launcher-ready and is reconstructed and compiled with
LuaJIT after packaging before publication.
