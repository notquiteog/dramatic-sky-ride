## Dramatic Sky Ride 0.2.8

This Generation II compatibility update aligns terrestrial mount access with
Pokemon Gold's native Bicycle rules.

### Fixed

- Ground Ride now dismounts automatically after entering indoor buildings or
  dungeons through native Gold door warps.
- Ground Ride remains active through towns, routes, caves, gatehouses and
  seamless passages between maps.
- Amphibious Suicune remains available on water and is not mistaken for an
  invalid indoor mount.

The attached ZIP is launcher-ready and is reconstructed and compiled with
LuaJIT after packaging before publication.
